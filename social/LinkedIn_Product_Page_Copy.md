OpenBalancer addresses the unique challenges of modern cloud-native architectures and AI applications:

Key Capabilities:
• Smart AI & Model Inference Routing: Distribute inference workloads across OpenAI, Claude, vLLM, and local Ollama nodes with queue and latency awareness.
